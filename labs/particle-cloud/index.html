Review notes

What this is

A drop-in replacement for the wireframe blob on the home page, built from the frame-by-frame teardown of the reference clip. Sweep the pointer through the shell: the particles it crosses are thrown loose, orbit the centre, and settle back into place over a few seconds. Scroll down and the shell scatters from the top; scroll back and it reforms.

What is not in it

No bloom pass (the halo comes from additive blending alone), and no separate galaxy or brain shapes. Both can be added if this direction is approved.
